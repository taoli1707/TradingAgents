## How to read this

- A pattern is only worth trading if it is **selected**, its validation and test columns agree in sign, its DSR is high, and the trade count is large enough for your account to compound.
- Patterns that look great in train and die in valid/test are the normal outcome of data mining; the engine shows them so you can see what was tried.
- Everything here is gross of taxes and assumes fills at the next open at the stated cost.
